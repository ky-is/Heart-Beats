Heart Beats turns your favorite songs into playlists by artist, genre or year, ready to shuffle with a tap.

• Built from your favorites: songs you've favorited in Apple Music are grouped by artist and genre automatically. Songs rated 5 stars count too.
• Find more to love: favorite your most played songs right in the app, and your playlists grow with them.
• Years in Review: the songs you've played most from each year.
• Shuffle anywhere: ask Siri, use Shortcuts, or add the Most Played widget.
• Private: everything is built from the music library on your device, with no accounts or tracking.

Requires at least 100 favorited or 5-star songs in your Music library.
